The new recipes of our special dish

Pizza are a one of the famos food in planet .There are so many compant in the world racing to become the best berger making .Today we will teach you how to make ti in your house with an easy way

Ingredients:

  • 3 cups warm water
  • 60g yeast
  • 1,200g pizza flour
  • 3 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1.5 teaspoons sugar
  • 1.5 teaspoons coarse salt

Steps:

    Crumble the yeast, then dissolve it in the warm water.Add the flour, oil, sugar, and salt. Knead the dough for about 8 minutes until it becomes smooth and elastic. Cover the dough with plastic wrap and let it rest in a warm place for 30 minutes. Knead the dough on a work surface and shape it into a large ball. Let the dough rise, covered with plastic wrap, for 30 minutes. Preheat the oven to 275°C and place a baking tray inside. Press and shape the dough into a pizza base (approximately 35 cm in diameter). Place the base on parchment paper and add your toppings. Transfer the pizza onto the hot tray and bake on the middle rack at 275°C for 12–14 minutes, until the edges are crispy and the toppings are golden brown.
