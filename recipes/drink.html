Description:

The new recipe of our special drink

The perfect taste doesn't complete without a drink.So this is an easy and quick recipes for the best orange juice

Ingredients:

  • Fresh oranges (as many as needed)
  • Cold water (about ½ cup, optional)
  • Sugar or honey (2 tablespoons, optional)
  • Ice cubes (optional)

Steps:

  1. Wash the oranges, peel them, and remove the white pith and seeds.
  2. Place the orange pieces in a blender with cold water, ice cubes, and sugar or honey.
  3. Blend on medium speed for about 1 minute until smooth.
  4. Strain the juice through a sieve, pour into glasses, and serve cold.
