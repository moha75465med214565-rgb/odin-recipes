Description:

The new recipes of our special dish

Burger are a one of the famos food in planet .There are so many compant in the world racing to become the best berger making .Today we will teach you how to make ti in your house with an easy way

Ingredients:

  • Prepared hamburger patties
  • Hamburger buns (quantity as needed)
  • Lettuce
  • Yellow cheese slices
  • 3 tomato slices
  • Potatoes
  • Pickled cucumbers
  • Sauce ingredients:
    • 2 tablespoons yogurt 2 tablespoons mayonnaise Pinch of salt 1 tablespoon tahini

Steps:

  1. Place the hamburger patties on a baking tray and cook in the oven over low heat for half an hour until done and browned. Once ready, remove them and start assembling the burgers.
  2. Assemble the layers inside the hamburger bun: add a spoonful of sauce, a lettuce leaf, the patty, then the tomato slices. Place pickle slices on the sides, and finally, the cheese.
  3. Next, prepare some french fries to serve alongside the burger and sauce. Give this a try—homemade tastes so much better! 😋😋🥰🥰🥰
  4. Bon appétit! If you prefer a smaller version, try making it on toast; the bread gets crispy and the cheese melts—mmm, truly delicious! 😁😁😄😄😄
